Put all your images and videos in this folder, then reference them in
content/site.json as "/assets/your-file.jpg".

Recommended formats & sizes (keeps the PageSpeed score at 100):
- Portrait photo:      720x720 or larger square, JPG/WebP under 150 KB
- Reel thumbnails:     540x960 (9:16), WebP under 100 KB each
- Video thumbnails:    1280x720 (16:9), WebP under 150 KB each
- Category images:     640x400, WebP under 80 KB each
- og-image.jpg:        1200x630 JPG (social share preview)
- Videos: prefer YouTube links in site.json (loaded only on click),
  or .mp4 files placed here (H.264, under ~10 MB each).
